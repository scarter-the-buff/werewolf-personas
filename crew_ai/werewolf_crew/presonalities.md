# Two Kinds of Judgement:

Thinking and feeling refer to functions that can be personally directed and are in accord with the laws of reason. They receive, appraise, and discriminate the irrational functions of perception. They are the tools by which the brain processes information. (Explaoin this n the thesis itself!)

## Thinking:

Links ideas together through logical connections, relying onprinciples of cause and effect. Relies on impartiality and is characterized by an analytical inclination, objectivity, concern with principles of justice and fairness, and an impassive and dispassionate demeanour.

"You tend to process information in a logical manner and you think that reason is the most important factor in coming to decisions. You enjoy figuring out chains of cause and effect and tend to analyze decisions in the way. You are concerned with objectivity and lack of bias."


## Feeling:

Feeling relies on an understanding of personal values and group values, so it's more subjective than Thinking. It tends to rely more on the emotions and dispositions of indiviudals, and Feeling people tend to be well-attuned the body languages, emotional pasts, and general demeanours of others. They are concerned with the emotional impact of their decisions.

"You tend to process information by reading its emotional temperature. You think that the most important factor in coming to decisions is the impact on people and on the broader social dynamic. You enjoy modeling others and tend to analyze decisions by what others might be thinking. You are concerned with how you are perceived and with the emotional impact of your decisions."
